## Room — 8.5
Ocean view, very clean, with an Apple TV and 24/7 room service. A nice room — but not as luxurious as $1,300 a night should feel.

## Food & Drinks — 8.5
Casa de los Artistas is the only room type with access to every restaurant across the whole resort; the other houses are limited to their own restaurants, about 4 each. The Mercado buffet is big and good, and the grill station is the best part. Specialty restaurants have premium items like octopus. The pre-made desserts look great but are average; the ice cream and crepes are excellent. Kibi Kibi, the house's specialty restaurant, wasn't our favorite. The misses were about taste, not ingredient quality.

## Beach & Pool — 9
Around 50 pools, from ones right outside the rooms to big ones by the beach, plus a man-made saltwater river for paddling, with sandy patches. The beach itself is small and rocky, with no volleyball.

## Activities — 10
Most Xcaret parks are included; ATVs are extra (about $70 USD per person). There's paddling on the river, padel and tennis courts, a water park with slides at Xcaret México, an arcade, a kids club, a teen area, and a speakeasy and cantina at night. Fun, but not a party scene. The best activities of any hotel in the region.

## Service — 8
The staff are excellent: they walk you to your lobby, and one employee walked us 20 minutes to the padel courts. The butlers that come with Casa de los Artistas were a letdown across the 2–3 we had. They suggested only one activity, listed only the house's 4 restaurants, and never mentioned the resort-wide access.
