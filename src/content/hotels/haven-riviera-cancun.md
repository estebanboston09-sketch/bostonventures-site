An adults-only resort just outside the Hotel Zone. I stayed with the Serenity package in a swim-out room.

## Room — 9.5
Everything felt new and clean: a beautiful bathroom with a vanity, a shower big enough for two, a big comfy bed, and a mini fridge and bar with snacks and drinks, all included and refilled. Room service is 24/7 at no extra cost, delivered through a hatch so it never sits outside your door. And you walk straight out of the room into the pool.

## Service — 10
Top tier from check-in on. With Serenity you get your own check-in area, a welcome drink and a butler who gave us his WhatsApp and actually answered morning and night.

## Beach & Pool — 9
Three pools: a main one with music, DJs and activities, a calm one with no music, and a Serenity-only one. The Serenity beach area has better loungers and waiters who bring you food and drinks. I'd like a few more beach activities.

## Food & Drinks — 9.5
A sports bar, Mexican steakhouse, coffee shop, Asian fusion, Mediterranean, the buffet and a Serenity-only restaurant above the pool. Everything I tried was really good, the drinks weren't watered down, and the buffet ice cream is one of the best I've ever had.

## Activities & Vibe — 9.5
Serenity includes a five-course dinner with a show, followed by the cabaret, which has a different show every night. During the day there's pool volleyball and other activities (the schedule is in the hotel app), paddleboards on the beach, and snorkel tours for about $5. The spa was amazing — Serenity includes 30 minutes of hydrotherapy.
