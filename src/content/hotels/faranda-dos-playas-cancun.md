The cheapest all-inclusive I could find in Cancún, consistently around $200 a night for two. I wanted to see if saving money ruins your vacation.

## Service — 6
Check-in was a bit chaotic: a group was blasting music in the lobby and nobody said anything. We carried our own bags. Staff everywhere were friendly and smiling, just nothing beyond the basics.

## Room — 5.5
Clean bathroom, working A/C, a TV and a balcony with a lagoon view. But the pillows had yellow stains, the mattress felt cotton-filled, and the fridge was old and rusty. The bed is the most important thing in the room, so that dragged the score down.

## Beach & Pool — 7
The water is crystal-clear blue and there are nice hammocks. But the beach is tiny and rocky, and right next to a public entrance and the ferry, so it's busy with locals. The main pool is round and decent; the small one looked yellowish.

## Food — 5
There's variety — buffet, sushi, pizza, pasta, hot dogs and ice cream — and the ice cream was surprisingly good. But most of the buffet was poor, and some things I couldn't eat. I couldn't even find the alcohol brands online.
