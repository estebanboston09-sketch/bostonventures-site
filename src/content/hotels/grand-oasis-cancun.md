I stayed at The Pyramid, the upgraded part of the Grand Oasis Cancún. The facilities are shared, but Pyramid guests get a better room and access to newer, better restaurants.

## Room — 6.5
A standard room with a lagoon view and a bit of ocean to the left. Clean beds, comfy pillows, a tub big enough for two, and a mini bar refilled daily (snacks, water, soda, beer). It's not dirty, just a hotel that's been around a while. The decorative pillows smelled really bad, though.

## Food — 7
The main buffet was about a 5: missing options, some food cold or room temperature. Bites, the restaurant only Pyramid guests can use, was much better — get the sandwiches, skip the ribs. The big problem: at dinner time almost every restaurant was full with an hour wait, so we ended up at the buffet.

## Beach, pool & entertainment — 8.5
The beach is big with very blue water and little seaweed when we went in July. But there aren't enough free chairs, and the nicer cabanas cost about 1,900 pesos. The pool is huge and full of palm trees, with peacocks walking around — but in the afternoon it's packed, so go early. The entertainment was the best part: a fire show in the Pyramid lobby, the only casino inside a Hotel Zone hotel, and a surprisingly good dance show at the hotel's nightclub.

## Service
People were friendly but not well trained. We were told we could book restaurants later, then later told we couldn't. Security didn't know where restaurants were, and one morning a staff member walked into our room without knocking.
