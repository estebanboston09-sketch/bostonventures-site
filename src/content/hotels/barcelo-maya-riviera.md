A big resort complex south of Playa del Carmen. Staying in one Barceló lets you walk to and eat at any of the 14 restaurants across all of them. We stayed in March 2024 with Premium Level.

## Room
A long room with a huge closet, a steamer, a clean bathroom and a shower that fits two. The bed is big and soft. The mini bar is basic, but room service is 24 hours, and the balcony has its own jacuzzi with an ocean view (some rooms face the gardens instead).

## Food
The Premium Level restaurant is quieter, with good salmon and better desserts. The main buffet is huge — almost everything you can imagine — and above average, about 7.5–8 out of 10, but very busy. The à la carte restaurants (Japanese, Italian, French, Mexican) need reservations. We tried the French one: amazing onion bread, escargot, onion soup and big portions of ribs. Desserts were the weak point.

## Beach & pool
The beach is huge with lots of seating, and even in busy March it didn't feel crowded. The pool is one of the biggest in the Riviera Maya, split into four infinity sections with massage jets, in-water loungers, a quiet pool bar and a music bar with a DJ.

## Spa
Premium Level gave us 40% off, so a couple's massage was $180 instead of $300. The massage was very good and the place is calm. The "hydrotherapy" is basically the same jets as the pool.
