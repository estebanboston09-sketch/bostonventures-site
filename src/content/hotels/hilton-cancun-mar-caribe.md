## Room — 9.5
Recently remodeled, very clean, with a comfortable bed, a soaking tub, a big separate shower, a mini-fridge with drinks, a decent TV and a balcony with two loungers. I had a partial ocean and pool view. It's not huge, but plenty for two.

One catch: rooms facing the restaurant entrances hear the sliding doors all day, so ask for a room away from them.

## Food & Drinks — 9
There are 8 restaurants and 12 dining venues in total. The Vela buffet is fine; the grill station is the best part. The must-do is **La Luce**, the Italian restaurant with glass walls facing the lagoon at sunset — reserve ahead. Azulinda café is open 24/7 for ice cream, crepes and sandwiches. Drinks are mid-tier (Hornitos, Bacardi), fair for the price, and waiters come to you at the pools and on the beach.

## Beach & Pool — 9
Two large main pools, a kids' pool with a water-park playground and tipping bucket, and small adults-only hot tubs on the edge with ocean views. The pool bar does burgers, hot dogs and fruit. The beach is down a set of stairs, with plenty of palapas and good swimming. There was little sargassum during my visit.

## Activities — 10 for families, 8 for party seekers
Supervised kids club, a teen room (ping pong, PS5, Nintendo Switch, big TV), pool activities, beach volleyball and a casino night with fake money. The full schedule is in the Hilton app. This is a family hotel, not a party hotel — hence the two scores.

## Service — 9
The main check-in was full, so they moved us to VIP check-in. The concierge booked La Luce for us. Staff everywhere were friendly and helpful; no complaints.
