A family all-inclusive near the Isla Mujeres ferry, about 40 minutes from the airport.

## Room — 9
Ocean-view room with two beds, a seating area, a big TV, lots of closet space and a hammock on the balcony facing the sea. The mini bar is better than most (Gatorade, beer, soda, Nespresso) and refilled daily, and room service is 24/7. The bathroom isn't big, but everything was very clean.

## Food — 8.5
The buffet is well-rounded and tasty, and there's a healthy bar with smoothies — the golden one was my favorite. The steakhouse and seafood grill, the beach food and the cocktails were all good.

## Beach & activities
Tiki bar right on the sand, waiters who bring food and drinks to your lounger, and very little seaweed in August. Kayaks, paddleboards and a morning snorkel tour are included, plus a water playground out in the sea. The Ultramar ferry to Isla Mujeres is about a 5-minute walk.

## Pools & kids — 9.5 for kids, 8 for adults
An adults-only pool, a pool with a waterfall walkway, a main pool with volleyball, water guns and massage jets, and a private pool for Elite members. The kids club takes ages 4–12, and you can book a nanny. Adults who just want to relax will love it; adults looking for more to do may want something livelier.

## Service — 10
No complaints at all — friendly from check-in to the beach, the spa and the kids club.
