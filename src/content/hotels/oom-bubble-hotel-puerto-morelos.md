A boutique hotel near Puerto Morelos where you sleep in an inflatable bubble in the jungle. There are only eight bubbles, and when we stayed only three were booked.

## The bubble
They're not glass — they're a plastic-like material kept inflated by air, so you go through a two-door airlock to get in. Each one has its own privacy wall. Inside there's a lamp, a coffee maker, a safe and water (expensive, about $10).

## The property
At night it's very chill: insects, animals and music in the background. There's one small restaurant with board games; the salmon was about $22 and a bottle of wine about $35–40. By day there's a small cenote, a pool filled with cold cenote water and a man-made beach with real sand. There are lots more cenotes to explore in the area.
